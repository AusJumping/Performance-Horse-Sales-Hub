BUSINESS CONTEXT

Performance Horse Sales Australia and New Zealand helps horse owners market and sell horses, and helps buyers enquire about those horses.

CURRENT REAL-WORLD WORKFLOW TO SUPPORT

The horse submission workflow works like this:
1. Seller completes a detailed horse marketing form.
2. The first form is intentionally obligation-free. Sally wants people to be able to enquire and fill it out before they are locked in.
3. On the form, the seller can indicate either:
   - I am happy to go ahead with the listing
   - I would like to speak with PHS first
4. Sally reviews the submission.
5. Sally may call the seller if needed, but she does NOT want the seller editing their original submission after the fact.
6. Sally wants to edit the horse information herself inside the dashboard.
7. Once she decides to proceed, she tidies the form into a cleaned factual document called the Owner Response Certificate.
8. She then uses the cleaned certificate to create the horse description.
9. She sends BOTH the Owner Response Certificate and the horse description to the seller for wording approval.
10. Ideally the seller should be able to initial, sign, or otherwise confirm approval.
11. After approval to proceed, there should be a second-stage listing/cost agreement step so the seller is clearly agreeing to fees and terms.
12. Only after approval should the system create the Google Drive folder structure for that horse.

The EOI workflow works like this:
1. A buyer fills out an EOI form for a specific horse.
2. The buyer gets an automatic acknowledgement.
3. The EOI is stored against that horse in the dashboard and in Google Drive.
4. The EOI should also be sent to the seller automatically.
5. Sally does NOT want to heavily filter EOIs before the seller sees them. She wants the seller to see the actual enquiries because they can reveal whether the horse is being marketed correctly.
6. The seller then needs to be able to indicate one of three actions:
   - I will contact this buyer
   - This is not a suitable match
   - Please ask for a rider video
7. If the horse is sold pending or in vetting, new EOIs can still come in, but the acknowledgement should mention that status and optionally say the buyer will be contacted if circumstances change.
8. Sally wants a summary document of all EOIs for each horse, but the system should not make the final buyer decision on her behalf.
