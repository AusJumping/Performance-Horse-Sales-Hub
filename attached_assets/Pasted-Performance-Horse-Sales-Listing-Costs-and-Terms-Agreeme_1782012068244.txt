Service and costs:
Cost: $100 upfront (PHS may choose to waive this, depending on the horse being listed)
PLUS 5% commission/ completion fee - minimum $500/ capped at $2000 - this is due when: a deposit is paid or vetting booked; the horse is leased or sent on trial; the horse is sold (by any means or channel); regardless of the channel, method or means which initiated the sale; three months has passed* (please see pro rata fee) - whichever occurs first.

This timing is because, at this point, we have done the work required for the sale and the results of vet checks, or the horse behaving as described at the second viewing, are completely beyond our control. 

In fact, if a horse fails the vet check or second viewing, our work load doubles, in order to re market the horse and sell it successfully. 

We offer a 10% discount off commission, when paid upfront. 

Please note prices are PLUS GST

Completion Fee is based on listing price. This is to encourage sellers to listen to advice and list at market price with full disclosure about potential vetting issues - which makes sales efficient and straightforward. 

If a deposit is retained by a seller, in the event of a sale not proceeding, the completion fee is again payable as above.

In the event of the first sale not proceeding and the deposit being returned to the ‘buyer’, a $500 completion fee payable when the horse is finally sold. 
Pausing the marketing due to illness or injury of horse or rider - if PHS is sent a vet or medical certificate, indicating that the listing must be paused, a $500 progress fee is payable. This fee will be deducted from the commission or administration fee.

*Pro Rata Administration Fee - if the horse’s listing is confirmed by the owner and PHS begins the marketing process and the horse is, subsequently: no longer available for sale; no longer for sale exclusively by PHS for any reason; not able to be viewed within 7 days of viewing request; found to be ‘not as described by the seller’; or the horse doesn’t sell within 3 months/ 90 days and the seller is not following PHS advice re marketing and pricing - a pro rata administration fee is payable, based on days listed by PHS. 
The pro rata calculation: (5% commission ($500-$2000) ÷ by 90) x the days listed with PHS (up to 90 days) 
A minimum charge of $500 is due, regardless of calculation, to cover our costs and work done.

Please note that similar services charge 10% not capped. We keep our fees low to be fair to sellers but it means,  in order to be viable, we require every seller to pay for the work we do.
Please note prices are PLUS GST.

What happens next:
1. PHS will contact the seller to answer any questions.
2. Seller signs the 'Costs and Terms Agreement' to confirm that they wish to proceed with the listing.
3. Invoice will be sent to the seller.
4. Seller sends PHS photos and video of the horse.
5. Once invoice has been paid, PHS drafts the horses ad and portfolio.
6. The draft is sent to the seller for editing and approval.

Please note - horses are usually listed publicly in under 12 hours of this form being signed, depending on how fast the seller  sends photos and videos and approves portfolio text.

7. PHS markets the horse privately, directly as well as via social media and website ads/ posts.
8. PHS sends all viewing application forms to the seller and/ or provides feedback re the sale.
9. If seller wishes to proceed with a potential viewer/ buyer,  PHS facilitates contact regarding phone call, viewing or purchase.
10. PHS provides advice, if required, and facilitates the process.
